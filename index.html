Welcome to EchoSim

A schematic 3D heart you scan like a real echo machine. Three panels work together:

  • 3D Anatomy — a beating heart. The cyan fan is the ultrasound plane; drag to orbit.
  • Echo Display — exactly what that plane sees, as a live 2D ultrasound with colour & spectral Doppler.
  • Console — pick a standard view, choose a pathology, then move the probe/plane sliders and watch both panels update.

Keys: Space play/pause · ←/→ scrub · 1–5 views · R reset plane · L labels · C challenge

Measurements & severity thresholds are calibrated to BSE/ASE reference ranges.

    How to write the report

    1. Enter height, weight and sex first. Chamber volumes and LV mass are indexed to body surface area, aortic dimensions to height. Reference intervals are sex-specific.
    2. Report structure by structure in a fixed order: LV (size, wall thickness, systolic and diastolic function, regional wall motion) → RV → atria → valves (aortic, mitral, tricuspid, pulmonary) → aorta → pericardium → IVC / RA pressure.
    3. Measure at the right frame. End-diastole is the frame before the mitral valve closes; end-systole is the frame before it opens, where the aortic valve has just closed.
    4. Quote LVEF as a number (e.g. "LVEF 42%") and avoid "mild" or "moderate" LV dysfunction. BSE bands: ≥55 normal, 50–54 borderline low, 36–49 impaired, ≤35 severely impaired.
    5. Grade with the graded intervals (normal / mild / moderate / severe) and say what the number is: "moderately dilated LV (LVEDVi 95 mL/m²)".
    6. Valve disease is graded on concordant measures: AS on Vmax, mean gradient and AVA; MS on valve area and mean gradient; regurgitation on an integrated assessment (regurgitant fraction / volume, vena contracta, jet, flow reversal).
    7. The conclusion answers the clinical question: list abnormal findings first, most important first, each with the number that supports it. Compare with a previous study if there is one.

    Reference intervals: Harkness A et al. Normal reference intervals for cardiac dimensions and function for use in echocardiographic practice: a guideline from the British Society of Echocardiography. Echo Res Pract 2020;7(1):G1–G18. Right heart: Zaidi A et al. Echo Res Pract 2020;7(1):G19–G41. The intervals come from a largely Caucasian population and are not valid for 3D measurements, athletes or pregnancy.
